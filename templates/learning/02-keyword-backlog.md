# {{SKILL_TITLE}} Keyword Backlog

## Backlog Rules

* Do **not** immediately study every newly discovered keyword.
* Add each keyword to the backlog first.
* Study only a limited number of keywords each day.
* Recommended daily limit: **3 to 5 keywords**.
* Prioritize keywords based on prerequisites, practical value, and dependency relationships.

The backlog is a planning tool, not a checklist of everything you have encountered.

---

# Priority Definitions

## Critical

Required to understand the rest of the skill.

Without these topics, meaningful progress is difficult.

---

## High

Frequently used in practice or necessary for day-to-day engineering work.

---

## Medium

Useful knowledge that can be learned after the core concepts.

---

## Low

Advanced, specialized, or optional topics.

These can usually be postponed until the main learning objectives have been completed.

# Promotion Rules

A keyword should be promoted to a higher priority when:

* It blocks understanding of multiple other topics.
* It appears repeatedly across different resources.
* It is required for practical implementation.
* It is a prerequisite for upcoming labs or projects.

A keyword may be demoted when:

* It is highly specialized.
* It is rarely encountered.
* It is not required for current learning goals.

# Backlog Maintenance

Review the backlog periodically.

During each review:

* Remove duplicate keywords.
* Merge related topics where appropriate.
* Reprioritize based on current learning goals.
* Archive topics that are no longer relevant.
* Add newly discovered concepts.

The backlog should evolve alongside your understanding of the skill rather than remain static.

---

# Completion Rule

A keyword can be removed from the backlog when it has:

* Reached **completed** status.
* Been applied in a lab, project, or system design.
* Been linked to its related topics and notes.

The backlog should contain only topics that still require meaningful learning.
